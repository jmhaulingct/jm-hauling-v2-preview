Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 29, 2026

JM Hauling respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information you provide when requesting an estimate, contacting us, or interacting with our website or advertisements.

Information We Collect

When you contact JM Hauling or request an estimate, we may collect information such as your name, phone number, email address, ZIP code, service needs, job details, preferred contact method, photos, and other information you choose to provide.

How We Use Your Information

We may use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your estimate or service request
  • Contact you by phone, text message, or email regarding your request
  • Provide estimates and pricing information
  • Schedule and provide services
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve our services, website, and advertising
  • Maintain appropriate business records

Calls and Text Messages

If you provide your phone number, JM Hauling may call or text you regarding your estimate, service request, scheduling, or related customer-service matters. Providing your information does not require you to purchase a service.

Information Sharing

JM Hauling does not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers or platforms when reasonably necessary to operate our business, process requests, provide services, or measure advertising and website performance.

Website and Advertising Technologies

Our website and advertisements may use services provided by third-party platforms, including Meta and Google, to measure advertising performance and understand how people interact with our website and advertisements. These companies may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable measures to protect information provided to us. Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain business records, resolve disputes, or satisfy applicable legal requirements.

Your Choices

You may contact JM Hauling to request that we update or delete personal information you have provided, subject to information we may need to retain for legitimate business or legal purposes.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
