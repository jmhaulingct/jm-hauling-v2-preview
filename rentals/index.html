Before you load

What can go in the trailer?

Ordinary household junk, furniture and household cleanout debris. Tell us about special items before loading them.

What is prohibited?

No hazardous materials, chemicals, paint, fuel, oil, batteries, asbestos, medical waste, explosives or unidentified liquids. No rocks, concrete, bricks, soil, asphalt, roofing or heavy construction materials without prior written approval and an agreed loading limit.

Can I move or tow the trailer?

Only JM Hauling may move or tow it. We confirm a suitable placement area with you before delivery.

What if I need more time or exceed the included weight?

Contact us before the scheduled pickup to request an extension. Extensions require approval and an agreed price. Weight overage does not permit overloading; your trailer’s safe payload and loading boundary still apply.
