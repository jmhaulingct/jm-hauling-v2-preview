Questions before you book?

How much does a pickup cost?

Small pickups start at $125. Standard household-junk load prices range from $195 for a quarter load to $650 for a full load. Weight, access and special handling can affect the quote.

Do I have to move everything outside?

No. Let us know where the items are, including stairs or narrow access, so we can quote the lifting and removal.

Can you estimate from photos?

Photos often help. We confirm the scope and final price before loading.

What can’t you take?

We do not accept hazardous materials. Heavy debris and unusual items need a separate discussion and quote.
