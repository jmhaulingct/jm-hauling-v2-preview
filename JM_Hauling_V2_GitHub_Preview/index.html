Quick answers.

What is included?

Standard removal includes lifting, loading, hauling, disposal and a broom-clean finish on accessible hard surfaces. We confirm any additional work before starting.

Do I need to be home?

Curbside and driveway pickups may be completed without you present after scope, access and payment arrangements are confirmed.

What about heavy or hazardous materials?

Heavy debris requires a separate quote and loading limit. We do not accept hazardous materials. Tell us about any unusual items before booking.

Can you quote from photos?

Photos often help us estimate. Larger or complex jobs may need a free in-person assessment. Your final price is confirmed before loading.
